MISSION 1 DEBRIEF: THE CRYPTOGRAPHY SHIFT

You built a variable, ran a sequential loop that choked against $2^{128}$ combinations, then executed a quantum superposition circuit. Here is what that means in the real world:

THE GOOD: UNBREAKABLE DEFENSE

Quantum Key Distribution (QKD): In classical networking, an eavesdropper can silently copy packets. In quantum mechanics, observing a qubit collapses it forever.

If a spy taps a quantum fiber line, the keys corrupt instantly and trip the alarm.

THE BAD: PASSWORDS HALVED

Grover’s Quadratic Speedup: Grover’s algorithm turns an $N$-step search into $\sqrt{N}$. This mathematically cuts symmetric passkey security in half.

The Fix: Double the length of passkeys. A 256-bit key against a quantum computer offers the exact security of a 128-bit key against classical machines.

THE SCARY: "HARVEST NOW"

Store Today, Decrypt Tomorrow: Hostile agencies are intercepting and storing petabytes of encrypted traffic right now.

They cannot read them today, but in a decade when fault-tolerant quantum computers boot up, every past secret will unlock.
