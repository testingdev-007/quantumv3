THE GOOD: UNBREAKABLE DEFENSE

Quantum Key Distribution (QKD): In classical networking, an eavesdropper can silently intercept packets. In quantum mechanics, measuring a particle collapses its state.

If a spy taps an entangled quantum fiber line, the keys corrupt instantly and trip the alarm.

THE BAD: PASSWORDS HALVED

Grover’s Quadratic Speedup: Grover’s algorithm turns an $N$-step search into $\sqrt{N}$. This mathematically cuts symmetric passkey security in half.

The Fix: Double the length of passkeys. A 256-bit key against a quantum computer offers the exact security of a 128-bit key against classical machines.
